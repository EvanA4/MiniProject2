### Inactivity patterns
A large gap spanning the entirety of 2020-2023 has almost no commits. Interestingly, the CoVID pandemic started around the time of this gap. In fact, [March 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_COVID-19_on_education_in_the_United_States) saw a large number of schools shut down due to COVID-19. Perhaps the pandemic brought this project to a stalemate. Throughout this multi-year lull, many small improvements were added, with half of them being build-related commits. Matthias H Hennig has been one of the leading developers of the project, suggesting the cause for the spike after the gap in 2024 might be because of them.

### Was the longest gap was easy/hard to interpret?
It's hard to guess whether COVID-19 should increase or decrease the number of commits made to GitHub repositories. So many people were locked indoors at the time--I can really see it going either way. Because of this, it's entirely possible something else was the cause for the gap starting.

It's also difficult to guess why the gap ended and a new spike began. Why would the project suddenly have a new release made after a multi-year gap?

### Likely reasons for inactivity and recovery
- Inactivity

- Recovery
    - New motivation for the project
        - A stockpile of new ideas for features
        - Project grew in popularity
