### Inactivity patterns
Only one excessively large gap is present in this project's history, after a many batches of commits. Only small batches of commits have been made since, suggesting the project was completed early and only needs maintenance.

### Was the longest gap was easy/hard to interpret?
I figure that the gap is due to the project being mostly complete and not needing many more features. Because of this, there was a void of commits at the time. The longest gap felt easy to interpret for this project. Quite a few of these projects have an insane number of commits in the first quarter or third of their lifetime, only to shrink to a handful of commits a year for maintenance.

### Likely reasons for inactivity and recovery
Inactivity:
- Low demand for features
    - Many commits were made during the beginning of the project, suggesting the project was almost entirely completed
- Burn out
    - It's possible such an intense number of commits could have burned out some of the developers behind the project

Recovery:
- Need for maintenance
    - Dependency updates
    - Build tooling
    - Documentation
