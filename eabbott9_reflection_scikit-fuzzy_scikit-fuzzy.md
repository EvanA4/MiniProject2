### Inactivity patterns
This project never really became inactive. Peaks were consistently reaching at least 10 commits every few months. Ironically, one of the largest peaks was around [the height of the COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_COVID-19_on_education_in_the_United_States). Four of the five commits before the longest gap included the term "GitWash", suggesting a large amount of work was poured into the feature. Most of the commits were made by two people with the same last name, indicating this may be a project worked on by two siblings. After the large amount of work, they may have decided to take a break from the project. The same two maybe-siblings recovered the project, possibly because they had newfound time or had taken a long enough break.

### Was the longest gap was easy/hard to interpret?
With such consistent activity, it's hard to interpret a singular gap, especially since there were quite a few gaps elsewhere.

### Likely reasons for inactivity and recovery
- Inactivity
    - Major release versions dropping
        - Gaps resulting from natural progress on new versions
- Recovery
    - Two developers with the same last name contributed a lot to this project. Perhaps having a small team (of possible brothers) has been what's keeping the project alive.
