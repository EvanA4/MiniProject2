### Inactivity patterns
The project started with a large spike of activity, only to fall to essentially nothing for about half a year. Then, another spike occurred. Since the second spike, about one commit is made a month to the project. None of the original developers were the ones who recovered the project, indicating that the original contributors may have walked away from the project.

### Was the longest gap was easy/hard to interpret?
This project felt straightforward to interpret. Again, many of these projects have a lot of commits in the beginning and quickly die off.

### Likely reasons for inactivity and recovery
- Inactivity
    - The original developers may have left the project.
        - After so many commits, maybe a target deliverable was reached.
        - After so many commits, maybe the original developers were burned out.
    - Only one commit a month is being made on the project.
        - After a target deliverable was reached, it's possible the project only needs maintenance to satisfy users.
- Recovery
    - It's possible the project grew in popularity again and more features were requested.
    - It could also be that it took time for newer developers to find and start working on the project.
